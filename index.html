Programming Robots through Natural Language: An Agentic AI Approach to Learning from Demonstration

Abstract

Teaching robots new skills remains a central challenge in human–robot interaction, particularly when demonstrations must be collected through physically demanding or time-consuming processes. In this paper, we introduce a novel framework that enables robots to learn stable and generalizable motion behaviors from zero-shot demonstrations generated via large language models (LLMs). Instead of requiring users to physically guide the robot or operate teleoperation interfaces, our approach allows a human to provide a natural language instruction, which is interpreted by a simulation environment to synthesize demonstration trajectories. These synthetic trajectories are then encoded as dynamical movement primitives that guarantee asymptotic convergence to the specified goal.
